Partnership Proposal

 ABD Global focuses on developing appropriate federal and prime-contractor opportunities. The proposed model is intentionally structured around verification first, pilot second, and expansion third.
 No federal reimbursement, referral volume, or contract award is represented as guaranteed unless supported by an executed agreement, authorization, or other appropriate documentation.
